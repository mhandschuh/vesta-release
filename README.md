# Vesta

Lists for Android: to-dos with dates, reminders and repeats, shopping lists sorted by section, and plain lists
(movies to watch, gift ideas), in your own folders. Add things in one line: "Dentist tomorrow 3pm", "Pay rent every
1st of the month", "milk, eggs, bread". Reminders ring on time, also when the phone is idle; tick them off or snooze
them right in the notification. No account, no ads, no tracking; your lists stay on your phone.

This repository only holds the installable APKs. Android 11 or newer.

## Install (with automatic updates)

1. Install **Obtainium** from its [releases page](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium/releases)
   (download the `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`, open it, allow installing from your browser when asked).
2. In Obtainium: **Add App**, paste `https://github.com/mhandschuh/vesta-release`, tap **Add**, then **Install**.
   Allow Obtainium to install apps when Android asks.

Obtainium checks for new versions and shows a notification; tap it to update. Your lists stay.

## First steps

- **New list** → To-dos, Shopping or Plain list. Folders (and folders in folders) keep many lists in order: hold
  a folder or list for its menu.
- Type in the line at the bottom; what Vesta understood shows above it. "Not a date" keeps the words as text.
- The first reminder asks to allow notifications: allow it, or reminders can't show.
